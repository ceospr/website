Performance definitions

The three trade-level ratios give equal weight to each completed trade's percentage return. They do not adjust for trade duration, position size or a risk-free return, and are not annualized portfolio Sharpe or Sortino ratios.

Trade return / variability
Mean trade percentage return divided by the population standard deviation of trade percentage returns.
Trade return / loss magnitude
Mean trade percentage return divided by the root-mean-square percentage loss among losing trades.
Trade return gain/loss ratio
Sum of positive trade percentage returns divided by the absolute sum of negative trade percentage returns. This differs from a profit factor calculated from dollar P&L.
